# A4 imposition and Tomus title page plan

## Context

The reader produces trim-size inner Tomi and separate two-up sheets for home printing and binding. Its sheets currently assume US Letter. A binder with A4 paper needs an A4 imposition option while cutting the same inner pages to the same trim size. The facsimile Tomus title page also attributes the book in a way that does not describe this Latin reader; it should credit Wilhelm Xylander for the Latin translation and Theodor Dœhner for revision, emendation, and ordering in centered, multiline capitals like the supplied nineteenth-century title-page example. The current facsimile Tomus I physical page 23 also ends after a short block in Theseus although the same sentence continues at the top of page 24; the earlier source-rule repair improved that page but did not fill it.

## Current state of the world

- `src/latex.rs` owns both the inner-page LaTeX and the shared imposition wrapper. `WRAPPER_HEAD` fixes the wrapper at US Letter landscape (27.94 × 21.59 cm); `WRAPPER_GEOMETRY` places two 10.5 × 16 cm trim rectangles at `Lx=3.47`, `Mx=13.97`, `Rx=24.47`, `By=2.795`, `Ty=18.795`. `wrapper_document` emits the same 32-page signature order, fold line, sew marks, and cut border for every Tomus. `write_imposition_wrappers` writes `tomus_N.tex`.
- The facsimile path (`src/bin/facsimile.rs`, `src/facsimile/mod.rs::FacsimileArgs`, `compile_and_impose`, and `write_wrappers_from_estimate`) calls that wrapper writer in its compiled and `--no-compile` paths. The transcription path also calls the shared writer, so its current Letter behavior and API need to remain intact. The facsimile CLI has no paper-size option.
- The facsimile title is emitted by `src/latex.rs::tomus_title_page`, called from `src/facsimile/assemble.rs::write_inner`. It has the old `SECUNDUM CODICES PARISINOS RECOGNOVIT` and `THEODOR DŒHNER` lines, followed by the Tomus numeral and a bullet-separated, small-caps Lives list. The optional ex libris plate is separate. The transcription path has a distinct `title_page` and `tomus_label`; the requested wording concerns the facsimile Tomus title, so those functions do not need bibliographic edits.
- Tomus I physical page 23 is Theseus body canvas 7. The current compiled PDF shows about eight Latin lines ending `in somniis`, then a large blank area; page 24 starts `gesta ejus occurrebant`. The previous rectangular `SOURCE_FURNITURE_MASKS` entry for source v1 p17 (`[1043,270,1053,2655]` on a 2006 × 2975 raster) raised that canvas from 54 to 432 pixels, and `backlog_theseus_orphan_line_rejoins_body` currently accepts 400 pixels. The fixture's current p17 bands still end in one joined band `[672,2651]` after nine separate earlier bands. Inspection of the 300-DPI source shows the column divider slopes right from about x1043–1047 at y280 through x1069–1072 at y1800 to x1082–1085 at y2650, beyond the rectangular mask's x1053 edge. The joined band is the leading hypothesis for the remaining premature wrap; Phase 4 traces the band through composition before making a code change. `src/facsimile/reflow.rs::band_source_column` controls line detection, and `compose_body_canvases` packs those bands across source pages.
- `src/latex.rs`, `src/facsimile/assemble.rs`, `src/bin/facsimile.rs`, and `src/facsimile/mod.rs` already contain relevant tests. `README.md` describes the present US Letter facsimile output. Existing untracked `tmp/` files are unrelated and must be left alone.

## Desired end state

- The facsimile binary accepts `--paper-size letter|a4`, with `letter` as the default. The selected value governs every facsimile imposition wrapper, including `--no-compile` output. Invalid values fail through Clap before build work begins. The transcription binary remains Letter-only.
- Letter produces the current landscape sheet and trim placement. A4 produces a 29.7 × 21.0 cm landscape sheet with the same 10.5 × 16 cm book rectangles, centered as a pair: `Lx=4.35`, `Mx=14.85`, `Rx=25.35`, `By=2.5`, `Ty=18.5` cm. The fold line, sewing marks, cut rectangle, signature order, inner PDF page size, and inner page content remain the same. PDF print instructions specify actual size/100% and duplex short-edge flipping for landscape sheets; the binder cuts to the unchanged trim border.
- Every emitting facsimile Tomus title page displays the words and punctuation of the user-supplied sentence, “Plutarchi Vitae a Guilielmo Xylandro Latine redditae, atque a Theodoro Dœhnero recognitae, emendatae et ordinatae,” as centered, deliberately broken all-capital lines. Use `Æ` for the `ae` display ligature, `Œ` in `DŒHNERO`, and the reference image's classical `V` display spelling for `U` in `PLVTARCHI`, `GVILIELMO`, and `ATQVE`. Retain the doubled `D` of `REDDITÆ`, which is in the user's supplied sentence. The Tomus numeral, existing bullet-separated Lives, and optional ex libris plate still appear once and fit within the trim page. This is editorial title-page text, not a change to the source transcription cache.
- Theseus source v1 p17 is banded as separate Latin rows through its lower body, so the sentence crossing the present page-23/page-24 boundary flows continuously and page 23 is substantially filled. A one-page exception excludes the divider-bearing left edge only from line detection; the original cropped source image is still used for emitted strips. The Latin rows survive once, in order, without changing the transcription cache, trim dimensions, or unrelated source-page behavior. The prior repair and its historical measurements stay recorded.
- Relevant focused tests, full `cargo test`, `cargo fmt --all --check`, and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` pass. A sample of both imposed page sizes and the title page is rendered and inspected where LuaLaTeX is available.

## Tech Learnings

The inner PDF defines the book page at trim size. The wrapper embeds those PDF pages at native size in a larger sheet; moving the wrapper's rectangles changes the available printer paper without reflowing Latin or changing signature pagination. A4 landscape is slightly shorter and wider than Letter landscape, so its horizontal and vertical offsets both need adjustment. The cut rectangle is 21 × 16 cm, leaving 4.35 cm at each side and 2.5 cm at top and bottom of A4.

The body compositor scales cut-point intervals formed from detected source-line bands. A nearly vertical printed rule can put dark pixels in every inter-line gap and turn many real lines into one band. For the Theseus page, a straight rectangular mask clears only the upper part of a slanted divider. Detecting bands from a narrower view of this page's crop, while extracting strips from the full original crop, can separate the lines without deleting printed Latin or the divider from the reader.
