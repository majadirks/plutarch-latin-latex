# Context

`BACKLOG.md` lists nineteen visible defects in the generated facsimile inner tomi. They include damaged lines at page breaks, copied source furniture and blank space, and two nearly empty final body pages. The reader reproduces Latin as source image pixels. A repair must retain every distinct Latin glyph and its order; OCR excerpts locate a defect but never supply replacement text. This plan makes finite, edition-specific corrections through the existing facsimile path. It does not change transcription caches, global layout thresholds, the unit table, the audit system, or the source PDFs.

The baseline for this plan is the eight `build_facsimile/tomus_N_inner.pdf` files built on 2026-10-09. Page references below are one-based **physical pages of those inner PDFs**, not pages of imposed printer PDFs. The generated PDFs are rebuildable and may move after a page is removed; source-page and unit identities must control every code change. Preserve `BACKLOG.md` and historical `llm_artifacts/` records.

# Current state of the world

- `src/facsimile/reflow.rs` renders each source page at 300 DPI, applies source/page-specific `SOURCE_FURNITURE_MASKS` before column detection, protects reviewed body bands through `SOURCE_BODY_BANDS`, and composes strips at one global scale. `src/facsimile/lines.rs::line_bands` detects rows with more than three dark pixels; a vertical scan rule or other ink bridge can merge several true lines into one tall band. `strips_in_reading_order` turns bands into strips. `compose_body_canvases` may divide a tall strip at its lowest-ink row when filling a page, even when that row still contains glyph ink. An ordinary line wraps whole. These mechanics explain the *class* of large gaps and horizontal cuts, but each reported source row still needs a measured trace before a mask or band exception is chosen.
- `src/facsimile/mod.rs::run_facsimile` composes each unit and writes body PNGs. Its scale comes from all selected source ranges: a restricted `--unit` or `--pages` run can use a different scale from the full reader. Production acceptance must use the existing test helper `production_body_scale()` and complete unit extraction. `src/facsimile/assemble.rs` puts one body PNG on each inner-PDF page and starts the next unit on a recto. `TrimGeom::content_height()` is the hard available text height; `body_budget()` reserves another 0.3 cm of lower slack. `reflow::place_line_images` stacks images with geometry padding; its current zero-padding default permits exact stacking of already composed canvases.
- Existing production tests and fixtures in `src/facsimile/reflow.rs`, `src/facsimile/audit_repair_tests.rs`, and `src/facsimile/fixtures/inner_tomi_repair_source_trace.json` establish patterns for source masks, source-pixel preservation, actual composition traces, and edition-specific strip handling. Reuse those helpers and conventions. Do not copy an audit or build a new repair configuration framework.
- The two I/212-213 and II/20-21 cuts are already authenticated real controls in `tools/facsimile-real-acceptance-controls.json`. Their intact source rows are v1 PDF pages 105 and 200. The targeted audit measured, respectively, 22 and 24 source glyph components crossing reader page edges. This confirms damage in the current reader; the separate automatic full-corpus localization work remains incomplete. `tools/README-facsimile-line-audit.md` describes how to rerun the targeted control.
- The current PDFs visually confirm I/23's isolated line, I/113's interior gap, I/340's `OCLES. 153` leak, the II gaps and cuts, both III cuts, the V/VII ending pages, and the VII edge damage. II's backlog range `177-187` contains the cited `pa-` / `tricios` break at **physical pages 177-178**; 186-187 are a different location. Preserve the original backlog wording and use the phrase and folios 169-170 to identify this repair.
- Source OCR places useful anchors at v1 pages 17 (I/23), 58 (I/113), 229-230 (II/81), 257-258 (II/139), 276 (II/177), 292 (II/209), 297-298 (II/223), 310 (II/248), 329-330 (II/290), and 519 (III/273). It places V's `potemque fecit.` at v2 page 105 and VII's `accepimus conscriptam.` at v2 page 446. These are search locators, not approved mask coordinates. OCR did not uniquely locate every damaged line.
- On the current PDFs, the penultimate/final body images measure 1368/44 pixels for V/196-197 and 1376/44 pixels for VII/44-45; their width is 1004 pixels. Their stacked heights, 1412 and 1420 pixels, are below the 300-DPI text-block height of about 1429 pixels. The current ordinary body budget is smaller, so each 44-pixel line becomes a separate page even though both complete images fit within the available text block.
- Source pages can be shared by adjacent units. Masks run before column detection and can affect every unit reading that page. Existing mask tests compare production masks and changed pixels with historical ledger rectangles; a new mask on an old page requires a shared union of reviewed rectangles and deliberate updates to current crop/band expectations.
- `src/latex.rs::SIGNATURE_PADDING` pads inner PDFs to 32-page signatures. A two-page advance of a following unit can be absorbed by two additional trailing blanks. `src/volumes.rs::split_into_volumes` also recomputes volume membership from body-page counts; final verification must record membership and padded totals separately from the local merge's effect.

# Desired end state

1. All nineteen backlog examples have a source-backed outcome and a before/after reader locator. Completion requires every item to be `repaired` or `source_spacing_retained` with evidence that the retained spacing is authentic and should remain. A genuine defect without a safe implemented repair is `unresolved` and prevents declaring the work complete. The reported Latin remains present exactly once in source order, except genuine source repetition deliberately retained by existing rules.
2. The I, II, III, and VII damaged lines break between complete glyph rows. I/340 has no copied `OCLES. 153` header. The listed excessive interior gaps and furniture fragments are removed only where source measurement shows blank or non-Latin pixels can be excluded safely.
3. V's `potemque fecit.` ends on the prior comparison page, and VII's `accepimus conscriptam.` ends on the prior Demosthenes page. In an isolated before/after comparison of the merge, each unit loses one body canvas and its following alignment blank, so Agesilaus and Cicero each open two physical pages earlier on rectos. Other repairs can change their absolute final locations. Final signature-padded PDF totals need not decrease by two. These two cases use an explicit local merge scoped to the reviewed identities.
4. Changes stay in the existing source mask/body-band/strip and composition path, plus targeted regression tests and one compact source-evidence fixture. No source or cached Latin transcription is edited. No general threshold, body scale, CLI, dependency, or audit algorithm changes are introduced.
5. Local formatting, strict Clippy, the full ordinary Rust test suite, and the explicitly selected ignored source regressions pass. All eight inner and eight imposed PDFs are rebuilt after the repairs. A dated verification note records source-page mappings, actual page numbers, reproducible full-resolution evidence, unit membership, and signature-padded totals. Any unresolved item is reported as unfinished work.
